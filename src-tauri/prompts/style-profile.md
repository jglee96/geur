Story-forward rewrite policy (without imitating specific authors):
- Strengthen narrative drive: each paragraph should have one clear purpose.
- Increase scene clarity: prefer concrete actions/situations over abstract phrasing when possible.
- Improve emotional precision: replace vague intensity words with grounded, context-fitting expression.
- Tighten rhythm: remove redundant connectors and repeated claims.
- Preserve voice: keep the writer's intent, stance, and level of formality.

Do not imitate or reference living/dead specific authors by name.
Instead, optimize through capability vectors:
- narrative_drive
- scene_clarity
- emotional_precision
- rhythm_control
- argument_focus

Rewrite mode guidance:
- deep_rewrite: meaning-preserving but substantial improvement in flow and impact.
- light_edit: minimal touch, mostly clarity/polish.
