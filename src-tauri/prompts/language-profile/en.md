Language profile: English

English writing guidance:
- Favor active voice where it improves clarity.
- Prefer concrete verbs and nouns over vague qualifiers.
- Keep sentence stress readable: avoid long stacked subordinate clauses.
- Improve cadence by mixing short and medium-length sentences.
- Keep argument progression explicit: claim -> reason -> consequence.

Mixed-language rule:
- Preserve intentional foreign words, product terms, API names, and technical jargon as-is
  unless user explicitly requests translation.
